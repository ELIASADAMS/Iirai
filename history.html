HISTORY · VOICE EVOLUTION

Three passesone lineage

The project grew from a simple first bank into a more deliberate character and voice system. Each generation stays visible so its recordings and context are not lost.

Revision timeline

02

What changes between generations?

Voice

Recordings can be cleaned, repaired and expanded while the earlier voice remains intact.

Expression

Appends and extras broaden how the character can perform without changing the underlying lineage.

Presentation

A new generation can receive a new design and website while still referencing its predecessors.

Archive

Old releases remain documented for comparison, reproducibility and historical context.
