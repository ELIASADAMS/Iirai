# 茜音イーライ (Akane Iirai) — Character

## Core identity

茜音イーライ (Akane Iirai) is a scruffy, bright-eyed tinkerer and trickster who treats reality like an editable project file. His oversized scissors are a defining motif: they cut awkward silences, bad takes and timeline seams rather than functioning as a conventional weapon.

## Visual identity

Messy orange hair, freckles, large greenish eyes and a slightly unhinged smile. His outfit combines mechanic, tailor and steampunk elements: goggles, gear motifs, tool belts, patched clothing and a loose tie.

## Personality

Hyperactive, talkative and playful on the surface, with fast rambling speech and glitched laughter. His singing voice contrasts with that behavior: warm, bass-leaning, textured and capable of a clear upper range.

## Likes

Forgotten drafts, abandoned prototypes, strange tools, half-finished songs, messy project folders, bass-heavy mixes, chant-like patterns, call-and-response vocals, children's games, late-night debugging, glitched laughter and accidental DAW time skips.

## Dislikes

Awkward silence, over-polished corporate production, permanent deletion, rigid workflows, unexplained folder changes, broken paths, deadlines that kill experimentation and being treated as a preset rather than a collaborator.

## Relationships

- ネラミ (Nerami): deep-sea companion who gathers his edit scraps.
- Fond of Kasane Teto and vFlower.

## Historical note

Akane Iirai is the updated version of the original イーライ (Iirai). The original character remains part of the project's history and should be documented as an earlier iteration rather than silently replaced.
