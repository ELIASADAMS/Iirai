# Usage Guide

## Current Akane Iirai bank

茜音イーライ is a Japanese CVVC voicebank with three pitches: A3, F3 and C3.

- Encoding: Romaji
- Alias format: CVVC
- Optimum BPM: 70–120
- Primary recommendation: TIPS for bass preservation
- Other recommendations: Moresampler, WORLDLINE-R, wavtool4vcv

## Recommended use

The updated bank is designed for expressive phrasing and is especially suited to fast or rambling lines, chant-like sections and subtle glitched ad-libs. Its warm bass-leaning core and clearer upper range make it useful both for low-frequency texture and more present melodic lines.

## Moresampler expressions

- Velocity: 0–200
- Gender: -100 to 100
- Tone Shift: -36 to 36
- Breathiness: 0–100
- Tension: -200 to 200
- Growl: 0–100

See `VOICEBANK.md` for the meaning of each parameter.
