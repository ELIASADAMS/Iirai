Updated Voicebank

茜音イーライ is the updated version of the original イーライ. The current bank has three pitches and improved phoneme clarity compared with the first iteration. Its warm, bass-leaning core now extends into a clearer, more confident upper range that can cut through dense mixes without losing texture.

Character

A scruffy, bright-eyed tinkerer who wanders through drafts and blueprints, editing reality with oversized scissors. Hyperactive and grinning on the surface, he rambles fast, snaps into glitched laughter, and treats timelines like messy project folders.

He is nicknamed 怪人ハサミ (Kaijin Hasami, “Scissor Kaijin”), a trickster who snips awkward silences and bad takes out of reality, leaving jump cuts, déjà vu and seams that do not quite line up.

Visual Design

Messy orange hair, freckles, big greenish eyes and a permanent slightly unhinged smile. His steampunk-ish mechanic-tailor outfit includes goggles, gear-pattern details, tool belts, patched clothing and a loose tie.

Voicebank Characterisation

The voice is designed for expressive phrasing, fast or rambling lines, chant-like parts and subtle glitched ad-libs. Recommended tools are TIPS (primary for bass preservation), Moresampler, WORLDLINE-R and wavtool4vcv.
