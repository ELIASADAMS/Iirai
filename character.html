01

Character

The oversized scissors are a defining motif for cutting through awkward silences, bad takes and timeline seams. He is hyperactive, talkative and playful, with fast rambling speech and glitched laughter. His singing voice is warm, bass-leaning and textured, with increasingly open upper-register expression.

02

Gender & expression

The voice keeps moving toward a place between the old categories.

The first イーライ form came from a more masculine vocal and visual origin. 茜音イーライ developed into a more feminine and queer expression. 暁 continues that development further, becoming more queer, more fluid and more deliberately gender-nonconforming.

The real voice provider and creator, Ilya Minin (Eli), has come to understand himself as non-binary and is finding a place between genders. The character's evolution reflects that personal artistic movement away from a masculine presentation that no longer feels representative.
